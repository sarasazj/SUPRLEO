Signal Chain

What the UPR system is demonstrating

Urban Passive Radar reuses existing Saudi broadcast infrastructure to detect LEO targets without transmitting a dedicated radar signal.

  1. 1Reference capture

    Receive the direct DVB-T waveform from the Riyadh TV Tower.

  2. 2Surveillance capture

    Listen for the same waveform reflected by the ISS or another LEO object.

  3. 3Clutter suppression

    Reduce strong direct-path and ground multipath components before detection.

  4. 4CAF / range-Doppler

    Correlate reference and surveillance channels to reveal delay and Doppler peaks.

  5. 5Detection support

    Use thresholding and classification to separate candidate objects from interference.

SSA Context

Why this demo matters

LEO is becoming harder to monitor. The debris facts below frame the problem, while the dashboard keeps the project focus on low-cost passive surveillance.

Tracked large objects >34,000

Objects larger than 10 cm are routinely tracked in current debris catalogs.

Small debris estimate ~900,000

Objects from 1-10 cm are difficult to track but can still damage spacecraft.

High-risk shell 700-2,000 km

Many long-lived debris populations occupy the same orbital region used by LEO missions.
